## 🎮 About me

I'm a **Level Designer** with a strong **Environment Art** background. I design spaces that are fun to play, readable to navigate and beautiful to look at, from the first paper sketch to the final lighting pass.

- 🧭 I think about **flow, pacing and player guidance** before I place a single prop
- 🧱 I iterate fast with **blockouts and playtests**
- 🌲 I bring levels to life with **set dressing, foliage, lighting and atmosphere**
- ⚙️ I keep everything **optimized and production-ready**

## 🗺️ What I do

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧭 Level Design
- Paper design, top-down maps and intent docs
- Blockout / greybox and metrics
- Player flow, pacing and critical path
- Landmarks, sightlines and guidance
- Combat spaces, cover and encounters
- Verticality, exploration and secrets
- Gameplay scripting (Blueprints)
- Playtesting and iteration

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🌄 Environment Art
- Worldbuilding and environmental storytelling
- Landscape sculpting and terrain materials
- Modular kits and set dressing
- Foliage, PCG and procedural scattering
- Lighting, Lumen, fog and atmosphere
- Materials, decals and vertex painting
- Composition and color mood
- Optimization (Nanite, LODs, HLOD, World Partition)

</td>
</tr>
</table>
