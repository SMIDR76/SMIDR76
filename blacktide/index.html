// THE SCAVENGER'S CHRONICLES

Nature and the Tide took Tokyo.
You Survive What Remains.

The BlackTide rose without warning. Bioluminescent, corrosive, and alive — it swallowed the coastlines and crept through every alleyway of old Tokyo. Manage scarce resources. Fortify your position. Extract before the Tide rises again. Every decision is permanent. Every mistake is final.

// SURVIVAL SYSTEM

Hardcore Resource Loop

Scavenge radiation-soaked ruins for food, fuel, and parts. Your survival depends on micro-decisions made under pressure with incomplete information.

// WORLD DESIGN

Drowned Tokyo

Every district — from Shinjuku's flooded metro to Akihabara's collapsed arcades — is hand-crafted from ground truth reference and environmental storytelling.

// THREAT MODEL

The Tide Is Alive

The bioluminescent fluid is not static. It responds to noise, heat, and light. Learn its rhythms or be consumed.

// EXTRACTION

Get Out Alive

Every run ends at the extraction point — if you reach it. Roguelite permadeath meets immersive simulation depth in a world with no second chances.
